# 🚀 Chekki AI - Future Feature Roadmap

The following features are prioritized for the next phase of development to increase user LTV and pedagogical value.

## 1. Digital Sticker Board (Gamification) 🍬 **(Top Priority)**

- **Feature:** Successful pronunciation or "Mastered" marks earn digital stickers.
- **Goal:** Drive daily active use (DAU) by making the _child_ ask the parent to open Chekki.
- **UX:** A 3D virtual sticker book with rare, themed sets (Space, Dinosaurs, K-Pop).

## 2. "Clone My Worksheet" 🪄 **(Pro Exclusive)**

- **Feature:** Take the structure of an existing worksheet but replace the words with new vocabulary from the same level.
- **Tech:** Uses Gemini 2.5 Flash Image to generate a new printable canvas.

## 3. Weekly "Mom's Report" 📧

- **Feature:** Automated email or push notification summarizing what words the child struggled with most this week.
- **Value:** Provides a sense of progress and accomplishment for the parent.

## 4. Academy Dashboard 🏫 **(B2B Expansion)**

- **Feature:** A portal for EK teachers to upload the week's curriculum, pre-loading the AI with context for even higher accuracy.
