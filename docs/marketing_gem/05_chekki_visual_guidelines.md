# 05. Visual Identity & Asset Cheat-Sheet (`chekki_visual_guidelines.md`)
**Chekki AI Knowledge Base — UI Design System, Mascot Vibe & Marketing Asset Inventory**

---

## 🏛️ Executive Summary

Whenever marketing content requires a "Visual Idea" description (for social media posts, ad concepts, or carousel slides), the proposed visuals must accurately represent Chekki's actual UI components, design aesthetics, mascot character, and available video/image asset library.

---

## 🎨 Color Palette & Design System Tokens

Chekki's visual system follows a modern, warm, high-contrast palette designed to evoke trust, calmness, and joy.

| Color Token | Hex Code | Visual Impression & Usage |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Chekki Indigo (Primary)** | `#6366F1` / `#4F46E5` | Trustworthy, technological, soothing. Used for main CTA buttons, header accents, and primary branding. |
| **Sunlight Gold (Secondary)** | `#F59E0B` / `#FEF08A` | Dopamine-triggering, warm praise, achievement badges, star reward overlays. |
| **Energetic Coral (Accent)** | `#FF6B6B` | Playful highlights, subtle attention badges, mistake markers in overlays. |
| **Night Card Surface (Dark Mode)** | `#1E1E2E` / `#2D2D3F` | Sleek, glassmorphic dark background reducing eye strain for evening parent study sessions. |
| **Light Card Surface (Light Mode)**| `#FFFFFF` / `#F8FAFC` | Clean, high-readability paper background for daytime review. |

## ✒️ Typography Guidelines

- **Korean Font Family:** Noto Sans KR / Pretendard (clean, highly legible, friendly rounded sans-serif).
- **English & Numeric Accent Font:** Nunito / Space Grotesk / Inter (playful yet crisp geometric numbers & headers).

## 🦅 Mascot & Character Vibe (`ChekkiMascot`)

- **Character Concept:** Chekki the Eagle — A friendly, wise, and warm bird companion wearing glasses and holding a magic feather/scan wand.
- **Personality & Vibe:** 
  - Encouraging, non-judgmental, warm companion (the antithesis of a strict red-pen schoolmaster).
  - Reacts with delight when the child pronounces words correctly or completes a worksheet.
- **Mascot Asset Varieties Available:**
  - `slide1_welcome_mascot.png`: Friendly welcoming stance.
  - `chekki-thinking.png`: Focused, analytical pose used during Gemini deep reasoning scans.
  - `chekki-happy.png`: Joyful celebrating pose with star rewards.
  - `chekki-actual.png` / `chekki-logo.png`: Main brand mascot vector icons.

## 🎬 Standard Visual Idea Templates for Copywriters

When suggesting a "Visual Idea" in post copy, follow these 3 high-performing layout templates:

1. **Split-Screen Contrast (Before/After):**
   - *Left:* Stressed parent at 9:00 PM squinting at a complex Poly phonics worksheet with a tired child.
   - *Right:* Calm parent holding phone with Chekki's Magic Scan active, reading a warm Korean script with a smiling child.

2. **UI Feature Close-Up:**
   - Close-up shot of a smartphone screen overlaying green visual ink answers directly onto a physical paper workbook, with `ChekkiMascot` giving a thumbs-up in the corner.

3. **Carousel Graphic (3-Slide Structure):**
   - *Slide 1:* High-contrast text hook over a warm lifestyle background of an open English workbook.
   - *Slide 2:* Product UI screenshot showing the [엄마 스크립트] feature in action.
   - *Slide 3:* App Store download screen with Chekki Pro badge and clear CTA button.
