# 01. Target Academy Context Guide (`target_academy_context.md`)

**Chekki AI Knowledge Base — South Korean English Kindergarten & Early Elementary Landscape**

---

## 🏛️ Executive Summary

Chekki AI operates primarily within the South Korean EdTech sector, targeting parents of children (ages 5–7 and early elementary grades 1–3) attending premier **English Kindergartens (EK / 영유)** and competitive after-school academies (_hagwons_). High-density education hubs such as **Daechi-dong**, **Mok-dong**, **Seocho/Gangnam**, **Songpa**, and **Haeundae (Busan)** form the epicenter of our primary user base.

Understanding the specific curriculum demands, vocabulary, and psychological pressures of these institutions is essential for crafting targeted, empathetic marketing campaigns.

---

## 🎒 Key Target Academies & Curriculum Profiles

### 1. Poly School (폴리아학원)

- **Academic Rigor:** Industry-leading reading and phonics volume. Heavy emphasis on AR (Accelerated Reader) points, SRI scores, and structured literacy.
- **Curriculum Speed:** Moves rapidly from single letter sounds to complex vowel digraphs (e.g., _oa, ea, ai_), diphthongs, and 3rd-grade American elementary reading materials by age 6–7.
- **Typical Weekly Homework:**
  - **Phonics & Grammar Worksheets:** 3–5 pages daily requiring sentence completion and rule identification.
  - **My Monthly Reading Log (AR Book Reports):** Summarizing main characters, plot, and new vocabulary.
  - **Spelling Practice Sheets:** Weekly lists of 10–20 words with spelling tests every Friday.

### 2. GATE (Great Minds Academy for Tomorrow's Elite)

- **Academic Rigor:** Gifted and talented focus, emphasizing critical thinking, creative writing, and STEM vocabulary.
- **Curriculum Speed:** Expects children to articulate reasoning in complete English sentences early on.
- **Typical Weekly Homework:**
  - **Writing Journals (Journal Prompt of the Week):** Open-ended response questions.
  - **Science & Social Studies Graphic Organizers:** Venn diagrams, cause-and-effect charts.
  - **Vocabulary Builder Packets:** Contextual fill-in-the-blanks.

### 3. PSA (YBM PSA) & ECC (YBM ECC)

- **Academic Rigor:** Balanced approach between academic phonics, communicative competence, and structured workbook drills.
- **Curriculum Speed:** Moderate-to-high pace with structured weekly themes (Animals, Space, Community Helpers).
- **Typical Weekly Homework:**
  - **Workbook Units:** Matching, word searches, fill-in-the-blanks.
  - **Daily Phonics Sound Drills:** Reading aloud lists of target blends.

### 4. LCI / Francis Parker / Apple Tree / RISE

- **Academic Rigor:** Play-based transitioning rapidly into rigorous academic literacy.
- **Homework Friction:** Heavy emphasis on home reading, audio listening logs, and weekly vocabulary worksheets.

## 😰 The "Double Stress" & Emotional Friction Points

Marketing content must directly validate the severe emotional and mental toll parents experience daily.

### 1. Cognitive & Time Fatigue (The After-Work Wall)

- **The Scenario:** Dual-income or busy parents return home at 6:30 PM exhausted. They have 2 hours to cook dinner, bathe the child, and check 4 pages of English phonics worksheets.
- **The Pain:** Decoding complex phonics rules (e.g., _R-controlled vowels_, _silent 'e' exceptions_) after a full workday causes extreme mental exhaustion.

### 2. Language & Pronunciation Anxiety

- **The Scenario:** Many parents did not attend English Kindergartens themselves or learn modern synthetic phonics.
- **The Pain:** They worry about pronouncing words incorrectly to their child, instilling bad accent habits, or explaining a grammar rule wrong.

### 3. Tearful Homework Battles ("엄마표 영어의 한계")

- **The Scenario:** When a parent tries to correct a child's mistake, tension escalates into crying matches, defensiveness, and refusal to study.
- **The Pain:** Intense maternal/paternal guilt. Parents feel like bad parents for losing their patience over a worksheet.

## 🎯 Strategic Marketing Positioning for Chekki

Chekki MUST be positioned **NOT as a replacement for the parent**, nor as a cold automated red-pen grading tool, but as a **mediator and partner**:

- **Core Tagline:** `"채점은 채키가, 칭찬은 엄마가"` (_Grading by Chekki, Praise by Mom_)
- **Positioning Statement:** Chekki takes away the burden of checking answers and finding explanations, handing parents warm Korean scripts so they can step in as the encouraging coach instead of the strict executioner.
