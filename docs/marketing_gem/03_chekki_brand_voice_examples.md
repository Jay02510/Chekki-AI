# 03. Brand Voice & Tone Bank (`chekki_brand_voice_examples.md`)

**Chekki AI Knowledge Base — Brand Voice Exemplars, Copywriting Principles & Anti-Pattern Contrast**

---

## 🏛️ Executive Summary

The Chekki brand voice is **Empathetic, Authoritative yet Accessible, and Action-Oriented**. We never talk down to parents, nor do we sound like a cold, dry textbook or an over-hyped tech SaaS product.

This document provides positive exemplars of "Perfect Tone" posts in Korean and English, alongside negative anti-pattern comparisons explaining why certain phrasing fails the Chekki brand standard.

---

## 🚫 Banned Terms & Phrases

Never use any of the following terms in copy:

- **Banned English Words:** `Revolutionary`, `Game-changer`, `Unlock`, `Delve`, `Synergy`, `Disruptive`.
- **Banned Korean Phrases:** `혁신적인`, `게임체인저`, `잠재력을 열다`, `깊이 파헤치다`, `혁명적인 AI`.

Replace them with natural, conversational language:

- Instead of _"Unlock your child's potential"_, say: _"오늘 저녁 숙제 시간, 칭찬으로 채워보세요."_
- Instead of _"A revolutionary AI app"_, say: _"퇴근 후 10분, 엄마의 마음을 가볍게 해주는 AI 동반자."_

## ✅ Positive Exemplars: "Perfect Tone" Copy

### Exemplar 1: Instagram Reel / Social Post (Korean - Empathy & Validation Hook)

- **Target Pain Point:** Mental fatigue and emotional friction during evening homework.
- **Hook:** 퇴근길 지하철 안에서부터 시작되는 저녁 숙제 걱정, 오늘로 끝내세요.
- **Body:**
  폴리 7세반 파닉스 워크북, 퇴근하고 돌아와서 꼼꼼히 채점해 주는 것만으로도 지치는 저녁.
  "엄마 이거 틀렸어?" 물어볼 때마다 어떻게 설명해야 할지 막막하셨죠?

  채키(Chekki)의 매직 스캔으로 워크북을 비춰보세요.
  1초 만에 디지털 인크로 정답이 표시되고, 엄마가 아이에게 뭐라고 힌트를 주며 칭찬해야 할지 '엄마 대화 스크립트'까지 한눈에 보여드립니다.

- **CTA:** 지금 채키 앱 다운로드하고 오늘 저녁 칭찬이 넘치는 숙제 시간을 만들어 보세요.
- **Why it Works:** Starts directly with validation without asking a rhetorical question. Mentions specific academy context (폴리 7세반). Introduces Chekki as a warm script generator, leading directly to an actionable CTA.

### Exemplar 2: Short Carousel / DM Script (Korean - Phonics Support Focus)

- **Target Pain Point:** Fear of teaching incorrect pronunciation or complex phonics rules.
- **Hook:** Silent 'e' 파닉스 규칙, 아이에게 화내지 않고 10초 만에 설명하는 방법.
- **Body:**
  "엄마, why is 'make' not pronounced 'm-a-k-e'?"
  갑작스러운 아이의 질문에 당황할 필요 없어요.

  채키의 상세 카드에 있는 [엄마 가이드]를 펴면:
  "마지막 e는 읏! 입을 다물고 A 소리를 길게 만들어준단다"라는 명쾌한 대화 힌트가 준비되어 있습니다.
  원어민 발음 버튼(TTS)을 눌러 아이와 함께 들으며 정답을 맞춰보세요.

- **CTA:** 프로 프로필 무료 체험으로 원어민 발음 코치를 직접 경험해보세요.
- **Why it Works:** Provides a real, concrete scenario every EK parent faces. Shows the exact UI solution ([엄마 가이드] + TTS button) and presents Chekki as an empowering tool.

### Exemplar 3: Ad Copy Variant (English - Dual-Income Parents)

- **Target Pain Point:** Lack of time and parenting guilt after a long workday.
- **Hook:** Stop spending your limited evening hours playing strict English teacher.
- **Body:**
  After an 8-hour workday, the last thing you want is a tearful 40-minute battle over phonics worksheets.
  Chekki scans physical worksheets instantly, giving you exact answer overlays and Korean teaching prompts.
  You handle the hugs and praise; Chekki handles the grading.
- **CTA:** Download Chekki on iOS and Android today.
- **Why it Works:** Direct, empathetic, and highlights our core motto ("Grading by Chekki, Praise by Mom") in clear, non-jargon English.

## ❌ Negative Contrast: Anti-Pattern Analysis

### Anti-Pattern 1: The Cold Academic Tool

> ❌ **Bad Copy:** "본 애플리케이션은 최첨단 OCR 기술과 딥러닝 알골리즘을 적용하여 영어 유치원 워크시트의 정답 판별 정확도를 99.8%까지 끌어올렸습니다. 파닉스 및 문법 오답을 체계적으로 분류하여 학업 성취도를 극대화하세요."

- **Why it Fails:**
  - Uses dry, technical jargon (`OCR 기술`, `딥러닝 알고리즘`, `학업 성취도 극대화`).
  - Lacks emotional empathy; sounds like a B2B software proposal rather than a supportive companion for exhausted parents.
  - Zero mention of the parent-child emotional bond or dialogue scripts.

---

### Anti-Pattern 2: Over-hyped AI SaaS Slop

> ❌ **Bad Copy:** "Revolutionary AI technology is here to disrupt traditional homework! Unlock your child's ultimate learning speed and delve deep into game-changing educational features with Chekki Pro today!"

- **Why it Fails:**
  - Packed with banned buzzwords (`Revolutionary`, `disrupt`, `Unlock`, `game-changer`, `delve`).
  - Sounds like generic AI marketing slop that parents instinctively distrust.
  - Doesn't mention a single specific feature or real parent pain point.
