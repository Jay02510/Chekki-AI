# Product

## Register

product

## Users

- **Primary**: Korean parents of young children (aged 5–7) attending English Kindergarten (EK) in South Korea (high-density education zones like Daechi, Mok-dong, Haeundae). They often face the "double stress" of validating complex English homework while managing parent-child friction at the end of a long day.
- **Secondary**: ESL/EFL teachers in South Korea hagwons (private academies) who need to plan lessons, generate listening worksheets, and save time on prep work.

## Product Purpose

Chekki AI transforms the "Homework Battle" into a "Bonding Moment." It allows parents who may not speak English to scan any worksheet, receive immediate answer verification, and get a warm, step-by-step teaching script in Korean. Success means parents feel confident and children feel praised and supported, rather than corrected.

## Brand Personality

- **Voice & Tone**: Empathetic, Playful, and Trustworthy.
- **Personality**: A helpful, friendly, and reliable companion (warm educational partner rather than a cold grading tool).
- **Emotional Goals**: Relief, confidence, parent-child bonding, and educational success.

## Anti-references

- **AI Slop / SaaS Clichés**: Avoid generic dark-blue dashboard templates, nested card layouts, overused Inter sans-serif typography, and standard purple-to-blue text gradients.
- **Cold/Academic Tools**: Avoid dry, red-pen correction marks, technical jargon, or patronizing language that induces anxiety for non-English-speaking parents.

## Design Principles

1. **Bonding over Correction**: The UI should emphasize parenting scripts and encouragement ("what to say") rather than just showing raw errors or grades.
2. **Empathy-First Microcopy**: Address user anxiety directly and without condescension (e.g., "You don't need to speak English to help your child").
3. **Paper-to-Digital Bridge**: Maintain physical-worksheet connection using skeuomorphic overlays (like high-fidelity digital ink and highlights) rather than extracting text into plain database grids.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- **Mobile Zooming (WCAG 1.4.4)**: Viewport must allow pinching and zooming so parents can easily read small text on photographed worksheets.
- **Bicultural Support**: Clear, readable layouts for mixed English and Korean typography, optimized for varying font weights (Noto Sans KR / Space Grotesk / Nunito).
