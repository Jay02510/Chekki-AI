# Chekki AI — Director Quick Start (14-day free trial)

Hand this to a director after a demo. Every step matches the live product; check `docs/PRD.md` before changing it.

**What the trial includes:** 14 days, 1 foreign teacher (FT) seat and 1 Korean teacher (KT) seat. No card or business number. Parents invited by the academy use the parent app free.

---

## Week one, in order

### 1. Director: create the academy (about 2 minutes)
- Go to **chekkiai.com/schools** and press **14일 무료로 시작하기**.
- Enter your name, academy name, email and password, then press **무료 체험 시작하기**.

### 2. Director: create a class
- On the dashboard press **+ 새 학급반 만들기**. Give it a name and level.

### 3. Director: invite your teachers
- **선생님 배정** → enter each teacher's email, pick FT or KT and their class. They get an email link, or copy the link and send it yourself.

### 4. Director or KT: invite parents
- **학생** → **학생 추가** (or **엑셀 업로드** for a whole class).
- Add the parent's email to send an invite. A student without an email is added to the roster only; add the email later from the same row.
- Tell parents: sign up with **the email the invite was sent to**. Kakao or Apple sign-in can use a different email and won't link to the class.

### 5. FT: upload this week's answer key
- **정답지 올리기** → photo or PDF of the answer-key pages. Parents' homework scans are graded against it.

### 6. FT: log the class
- **오늘의 일지** → type or speak a sentence or two in English. Chekki drafts the Korean parent report.

### 7. KT: check and send
- **학부모 리포트** → review the class's drafts, fix anything, send. Nothing reaches a parent without this step.
