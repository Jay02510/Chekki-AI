Chekki AI: AI Homework Help for Korean Families and English-Language Academies

Chekki AI helps parents check their child's paper homework in seconds using AI-powered worksheet scanning and pronunciation coaching, and helps English-language academies (hagwons) automate class logging and Korean-language parent reporting.

  • Scan any handwritten worksheet or academy workbook and get instant answer checking, powered by Google Gemini multimodal OCR.
  • Pronunciation coaching using North American English text-to-speech and speech recognition, calibrated for children.
  • Free tier: 3 scans per day. Pro tier: unlimited scans.
  • For academies: Foreign Teacher class logs are turned into Korean-language KakaoTalk parent updates, reviewed by a Korean Teacher before sending.
