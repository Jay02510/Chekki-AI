1Opener· Page 1

Listen, point and say.
잘 듣고, 그림을 가리키며 따라 말해 보세요.
He has… She has… Let's read!
Ben has a cup.Mia
Mia has a ball.Ben

has, he, she를 배우고 이 단원의 문장을 들어요.

2Picture Match· Page 2

Read and circle.
문장을 읽고 알맞은 그림에 동그라미 하세요.
Who has what? Look closely!
Mia has a ball.
Ben has an apple.
Mia has a cup.

누가 무엇을 가지고 있는지 읽고 맞는 그림을 찾아요.

3He or She?· Page 3

Look and circle He or She.
그림 아래 이름을 보고 He 또는 She에 동그라미 하세요.
Ben? He! Mia? She!
He has a ball.
She has an egg.
She is happy.
He is running.

he와 she를 구별해요: Ben은 he, Mia는 she예요.

4Picture Match· Page 4

Read and circle.
문장을 읽고 알맞은 그림에 동그라미 하세요.
Look at WHO, WHAT and the color!
Black-and-white edition
Read and color.
Color it! Read the color word.
Mia has a red ball.
Ben has a blue cup.
She has a yellow cup.
★ He has a green ball.

누가, 무엇을, 무슨 색을 가졌는지 읽고 비슷한 그림을 구별해요.

5Which One?· Page 5

Look and circle the sentence.
그림을 보고 알맞은 문장에 동그라미 하세요.
Read all three. Who has it?
Black-and-white edition
Read and color.
Color it! Read the sentence first.
ABen has a green ball.
BMia has a red ball.
CMia has a green ball.
AHe has a yellow ball.
BShe has a yellow ball.
CHe has a blue ball.
AHe has a red cup.
BShe has a red cup.
CShe has a blue cup.

그림에 맞는 문장을 골라요.

6Yes / No· Page 6

Read and look. Circle Yes or No.
문장을 읽고 그림과 맞으면 Yes, 다르면 No에 동그라미 하세요.
Look carefully. Yes or No?
Mia has an egg.Yes / No
Ben has a cup.Yes / No
The dog is in the box.Yes / No
She is sleeping.Yes / No

문장을 읽고 그림과 맞는지 판단해요.

7Find & Circle· Page 7

Read and find. Circle it in the picture.
문장을 읽고 그림 속에서 찾아 동그라미 하세요.
Detective time! Find it in the kitchen!
Mia has a cup.
Ben has an egg.
The cat is under the table.
The apple is on the table.

문장을 읽고 부엌 그림 속에서 찾아요.

8Say It· Page 8

Look, say and trace.
그림을 보고 문장을 말한 뒤, 단어를 따라 써 보세요.
He! She! Has! Say it loud!
Mia has a ball.
She has an apple.
Ben has a cup.
He has an egg.
Read it. Then draw it!
She has a red ball.
He has an apple.

문장을 말하고 단어를 따라 쓰고, 문장을 읽고 가진 물건을 그려요.

9Challenge· Page 9

Read carefully and circle.
문장을 끝까지 꼼꼼히 읽고 알맞은 그림에 동그라미 하세요.
Tricky! book… ball… Read it all!
Ben has a book.
Mia has a car.
She has a ball.
★ He has a cup.

문장을 끝까지 읽고 누가 무엇을 가졌는지 찾아요.

10Review· Page 10

Read and circle.
문장을 읽고 알맞은 그림이나 Yes / No에 동그라미 하세요.
You can read whole sentences now!
Black-and-white edition

Color it! Read the color word.
Ben has an egg.
Mia has a blue ball.
She is jumping.Yes / No
The ball is in the box.Yes / No
★ He has a small ball.
